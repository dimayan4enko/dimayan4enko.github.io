Privacy Policy

Application: claude-sheets. Last updated: 1 September 2026.

Overview

claude-sheets is a personal, single-user command-line tool. It is operated by its owner for their own Google account only. It is not offered as a service to other people and has no servers, no backend, and no user database.

What the application accesses

When you sign in with Google, the application requests one OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets — permission to view and edit Google Sheets spreadsheets in the signed-in account. It also receives basic profile information (name, email address) as part of standard Google sign-in.

The application does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive files other than spreadsheets, Calendar, Contacts, Photos, or any other Google service.

How data is used

Spreadsheet data is read and written solely to perform the actions the operator requests at the moment they request them — for example, reading a cell formula or updating one. Data is processed locally on the operator's own computer and is used for no other purpose.

Storage and retention

The application does not copy, collect, or retain spreadsheet contents. No spreadsheet data is stored on any server. OAuth credentials (an access token and a refresh token) are stored in encrypted form in the operating system's credential store on the operator's own machine, and exist only to avoid signing in repeatedly.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. Data is exchanged only between the operator's own computer and Google's APIs.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by removing "claude-sheets". Revoking access immediately invalidates the stored credentials.
